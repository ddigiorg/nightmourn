# Nightmourn

Nightmourn is a 2D action-adventure and survival game.

- **Fantasy**: A world of might, magic, and myth full of heroes and horrors.
- **Explore**: Traverse a colorful open world full of unique mystical environments.
- **Mine**: Collect resources, items, and treasures.
- **Craft**: Craft items like tools, weapons, armor, and spells.
- **Progress**: Level up your character with stronger stats and abilities.
- **Build**: Establish a kingdom, build settlements, construct structures, and recruit armies.
- **Survive**: Defend your kingdom and survive in a fallen world against an onslaught of evil.
- **Fight**: Rally the forces of good to destroy evil kingdoms and defeat legendary bosses.
